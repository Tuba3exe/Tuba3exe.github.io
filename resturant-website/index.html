Our Story

A Legacy Passed Down Through Generations

Graciela "Chelita" Ramírez, grandmother of Jessica started La Tapatía back in the 1970 's. Now it’s called La Chelita, purely family owned and run by the new family legacy keeper. La Chelita used to be a small tortilleria that served traditional Jalisco-style Mexican food and fresh handmade tortillas to the neighborhood's working-class families. For Chelita, food was more than something to eat. It was a way for people to come together as a community, celebrate family, and make everyone feel at home.  However, with Jessica being the new owner, she has to figure out how she can evolve the business to honor Chelita's legacy while introducing her own culinary vision shaped by growing up Mexican-American in Texas. As she reimagines the restaurant, Jessica is bringing together recipes she had grown up with alongside the culinary influences she was surrounded with. La Chelita honors the past while making room for more, allowing traditional Jalisco style cooking to meet the comfort of Tex-Mex cuisine through every family recipe, handmade tortilla, and shared meals, Jessica hopes to keep her grandmother’s legacy thriving while creating a welcoming home for the neighborhood's next generation. From Chelita’s family recipes to Jessica’s modern creations, every dish expresses a new part of art and history. Come join us for breakfast, a quick bite, a long afternoon lunch, or a nice family dinner.
